Tanpura

One real drone per Sa. Each recording's key is measured from the audio, not taken from its title. Tap a key to start or stop it.

Tabla bols

Single strokes, chosen from one uploader where possible so every bol sounds like the same drum.

Thekas

Played stroke by stroke from the bols above at any tempo, or as a full recorded loop where one exists.
